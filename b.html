Import test B tomato soup

Import test page.

Serves 4 portions

Ingredients

  • 1 onion, halved
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 20 g olive oil
  • 800 g ripe tomatoes, quartered
  • 400 g water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 pinch sugar
  • 100 g cream
  • fresh basil leaves

Preparation

  1. Place onion and garlic in mixing bowl and chop 5 sec/speed 5.
  2. Scrape down sides of mixing bowl, add olive oil and sauté 3 min/120°C/speed 1.
  3. Add tomatoes, water, salt and sugar and cook 20 min/100°C/speed 2.
  4. Add cream and blend 1 min/speed 5-8, increasing speed gradually.
  5. Stir in basil with reverse 10 sec/reverse/speed 2.
  6. To serve with steamed vegetables, place them in the Varoma and steam 15 min/Varoma/speed 1.
  7. Serve hot with bread.
